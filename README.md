# AI Curricular Exposure — MSU Denver AY2026–27

Interactive dashboard of the AI Curricular Exposure analysis: 3,194 undergraduate courses across six colleges and schools rated High / Medium / Low at three horizons (2028, 2031, 2036) for how far AI could erode the labor-market reason a student takes each course. Ratings revised September 21, 2026.

- **Not** a judgment of any course's educational value, quality, or future.
- Inputs are the public 2026–27 undergraduate catalog and published research only. No student or employee records; department contact details were removed from catalog text.
- Discussion marks, notes, and lists are stored only in the viewer's browser.

`build_data.py` regenerates `data/` from the analysis working files (not included in this repository).
