AI Curricular Exposure
Read this first. Each course is rated High, Medium, or Low on one question: how far AI could erode the labor-market reason a student takes the course, because AI will competently do the work tasks the course prepares students for. It is not a judgment of a course’s educational value, its quality, or whether it should be cut. Ratings come from five factor scores and a fixed rule, not case-by-case judgment. The analysis covers the MSU Denver 2026–27 undergraduate catalog and was revised September 21, 2026. How the ratings work ↓
